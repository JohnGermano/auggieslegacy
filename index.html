Auggie's Story

Auggie's story started on September 8, 2026. Auggie was born at Dublin Methodist Hospital and it was clear he was such a bundle of joy with a 'tude! He was a fighter from the start, and his journey took him to Nationwide Children's Hospital where he received the care he needed. His time with us was filled with love, laughter, and unforgettable moments that we will cherish forever.

Auggie spent 10 days in the Nationwide NICU, and during that time, our family was touched and overwhelmed by the kindness the staff at the hospital offered to us during one of the most difficult times of our lives. We are forever grateful for the care and compassion shown to Auggie and our family. Every year during the month of September, we will be celebrating Auggie's life and the impact he had on all of us. We invite you to join us in honoring his memory and supporting the hospital that made such a difference.

"Loved beyond words, missed beyond measure, remembered every single day."

Ashley and I are so grateful for the love and support we have received from our friends, family, and community. Your contributions will help ensure that other families receive the same level of care and compassion that we experienced during our time at Nationwide Children's Hospital.
