A Message From Our Hearts

Our Deepest Gratitude

When Auggie entered our lives on September 8, 2026 at Dublin Methodist Hospital, he instantly captured our hearts with his spirited personality and courage. His ten days with us in the Nationwide Children's Hospital NICU transformed our family forever.

During the darkest moments of our lives, the doctors, nurses, and staff provided not just world-class medical care, but genuine human warmth, dignity, and compassion. To experience that depth of support is something we will never forget.

When we launched this fundraiser in honor of Auggie's memory, we were overwhelmed by the outpouring of love, donations, and kind messages from our friends, family, and community. Crushing our goals together means another family in that very same NICU will feel comforted and supported tonight.

"Auggie showed us how much love a little soul can bring into this world. Thank you for helping his light shine bright forever."

With all our love and appreciation,

Ashley & Family

With heartfelt gratitude, Auggie's Parents
